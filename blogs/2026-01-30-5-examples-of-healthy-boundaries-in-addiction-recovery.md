If you’ve spent years avoiding conflict or have trouble saying no to loved ones, the early part of recovery can feel especially challenging. Navigating relationships and work can feel overwhelming and can trigger stress or cravings. Fortunately, setting healthier boundaries can help you protect your progress in recovery and ease the pressures around you.
